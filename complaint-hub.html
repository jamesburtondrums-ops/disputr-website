What happens next

1

Choose a company and issue

Search the provider list, select the type of problem and add the key facts you want the complaint to cover.

2

Review your draft

Disputr produces editable suggested wording based on what you entered. Check every fact and make any corrections.

3

Copy and send

Use the supplier’s official complaints route, attach relevant evidence and keep a copy of the final version you send.

Important information

Supplier contact routes can change. Where a company is shown in a searchable list, always confirm the displayed route on that company’s official website before sending personal information.

Suggested wording only: Disputr is a self-service organisation tool. It does not provide legal advice, claims-management services, financial advice or a guaranteed outcome.
