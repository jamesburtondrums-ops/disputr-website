Built for practical progress

1

Choose a route

Select the category, search for the supplier or company, and choose the issue that best matches what happened.

2

Build your suggested complaint

Add your facts in your own words. Disputr creates editable suggested wording and prompts you to check missing information.

3

Prepare to send

Copy the draft, review suggested evidence, and use the supplier’s official complaint route when you are satisfied with the wording.

Search suppliers and complaint routes

The complaint builders include searchable supplier lists for popular UK-facing companies. If a company is not listed, you can enter it manually and continue with your complaint.

Where available, Disputr displays the provider’s stored official complaints route. Always check the destination on the company’s own website before sending, as contact details and processes can change.

Suggested wording only: Disputr is a self-service organisation tool. It does not provide legal advice, claims-management services, financial advice, or guaranteed outcomes.
