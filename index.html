How it works

1

Choose a route

Select a complaint category, choose the provider and identify the issue that best matches what happened.

2

Add the facts

Provide your own dates, references, previous contact, evidence and the outcome you want the provider to consider.

3

Review and send

Edit the suggested complaint, check every fact, then use the company's official complaints route.

Company contacts done safely

Disputr can help you find a company in the relevant complaint builder and show its stored official complaint route. You can still continue if the company is not listed.

Contact routes can change. Confirm the destination on the provider's own website before sending personal information or attachments.

Suggested wording only: Disputr is a self-service organisation tool. It does not provide legal advice, claims-management services, financial advice or guaranteed outcomes.
