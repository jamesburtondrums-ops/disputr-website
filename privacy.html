Privacy notice

Last updated: 13 September 2026

This notice explains how Disputr handles personal information when you use the website, create an account, build a complaint, upload evidence, contact support or manage a subscription.

Information we collect

Depending on how you use Disputr, we may process your name, email address, account details, complaint information, company or organisation names, dates, reference numbers, case notes, reminders, uploaded evidence, support messages, subscription status and technical information needed to operate and secure the service.

Please avoid entering passwords, PINs, full payment-card numbers, CVVs, security answers or information that is not needed for your complaint.

How we use your information

We use information to provide account access, create and manage complaint cases, generate requested draft wording, store evidence and notes, provide reminders, respond to support requests, administer subscriptions, prevent abuse and keep the service secure.

Legal bases

Where UK data-protection law applies, processing may be necessary to provide the service you ask for, for our legitimate interests in operating and securing Disputr, to comply with legal obligations, or on the basis of consent where consent is specifically required. The appropriate basis depends on the particular processing activity.

AI complaint drafting

When you ask Disputr to generate suggested complaint wording, the complaint facts needed for that request are sent to the configured AI service. Disputr currently uses OpenAI for this feature. The generated wording is a draft for you to review and edit; it is not legal advice.

Service providers

Disputr uses third-party infrastructure and service providers to run the service. These currently include Cloudflare for hosting and related infrastructure, Stripe for subscription billing, and OpenAI for requested complaint-drafting functionality. Information is shared only as needed for the relevant service.

Payments

Payment-card processing is handled by Stripe. Disputr does not need to store your full card number or CVV. Stripe may process payment and billing information under its own privacy terms.

Evidence and uploaded files

Files you choose to upload may contain personal or sensitive information. Only upload material that is relevant to your complaint and that you are entitled to use. Access to case evidence is intended to be restricted to the authenticated account and the systems required to provide the service.

Retention

We keep personal information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain account and billing records, meet legal or security requirements and resolve disputes. Different information may be retained for different periods. Where deletion is available, some records may still need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate fraud, security or accounting purposes.

Security

Disputr uses measures including authenticated sessions and password hashing. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Use a strong, unique password and do not share your account credentials.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data-protection law may give you rights to ask for access to your personal information, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability or withdrawal of consent. Some rights are subject to legal exceptions.

To make a privacy request, use the contact page and explain what you need. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

Complaints

If you have a concern about how your personal information is handled, contact Disputr first so we can try to resolve it. You may also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice as the service changes. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
