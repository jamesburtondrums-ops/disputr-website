Using Disputr

What the service provides

Disputr provides self-service tools to help you organise complaint information, generate suggested wording, keep evidence and case notes together, and track follow-up actions.

Not legal advice or representation

Disputr does not provide legal advice, act as a solicitor or claims manager, represent you before a business or external body, or guarantee any result. You are responsible for checking the facts, wording, deadlines and suitability of anything you send.

Your information

You must only upload or submit information you are entitled to use. Do not use Disputr to store unlawful material or information that is irrelevant to your complaint.

AI-generated wording

Generated text is a draft starting point. It may be incomplete or unsuitable for your circumstances. Review and personalise it before use and verify any important factual or procedural point independently.

Subscriptions

Where Premium is offered with a free trial, the trial converts to the stated paid monthly subscription unless cancelled through the billing portal before the trial ends. Current pricing is shown on the pricing page before checkout.

We aim to keep the service available, but uninterrupted access cannot be guaranteed. Features may change as the service develops.
