Privacy notice

Last updated: 19 September 2026

This notice explains how Disputr handles information when you use a complaint builder, create an account, manage Premium or contact support.

Accounts and billing

When you create an account, Disputr stores your email address, a securely derived password hash, session records and account timestamps. Stripe processes payment details. Disputr stores Stripe customer and subscription identifiers, subscription status and billing-period dates, but does not store full card details.

Information used to create a draft

The builder sends the details you enter—such as the company, issue, dates, reference, description, previous contact, costs and requested outcome—to Disputr's drafting service. The current public beta does not save complaint drafts to a Disputr account.

Do not enter passwords, PINs, full payment-card numbers, CVVs, security answers or information that is not needed.

AI drafting

Complaint details are processed through Cloudflare's infrastructure and Workers AI to generate requested wording. A separately configured OpenAI service may be used as a backup. If both AI services are unavailable, Disputr creates a basic local template from the supplied fields. Generated wording must be checked and edited before use.

Support messages

If you use the contact form, Disputr stores your email address, message, submission time and a one-way technical identifier used to limit spam. Support information is used to review and respond to the enquiry and protect the service from abuse.

Provider directory

The selected provider and complaint route are handled in your browser. External provider links take you to a separate organisation with its own privacy practices.

Legal bases

Where UK data-protection law applies, processing is used to provide the service you request and for legitimate interests in operating, supporting and securing Disputr. A different basis may apply where the law requires it.

Retention and security

Complaint drafts are not stored unless a signed-in Premium user chooses to save a case. Saved cases remain associated with that account until deleted under the service retention process. Account, subscription and support records are kept only as long as reasonably needed to provide the service, handle enquiries, maintain security and meet legal obligations. Technical and organisational safeguards are used, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or portability. Use the contact page for a privacy request. Identity may need to be verified.

Complaints and changes

You may have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office. This notice may be updated as the service changes; the latest version and date will appear here.
