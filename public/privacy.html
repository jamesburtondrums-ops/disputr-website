Privacy

How Disputr handles your information

Information you provide

When you create an account or use Disputr, you may provide your name, email address, complaint details, case notes, reminders and files you choose to upload as evidence.

How it is used

This information is used to provide the features you request, including account access, complaint drafting, case management, evidence storage, reminders, support and subscription management.

Service providers

Disputr uses infrastructure and service providers to operate the service. This includes Cloudflare for application hosting and storage, Stripe for subscription billing, and OpenAI for complaint-drafting requests. Only information needed for the relevant feature is sent to that provider.

Complaint drafting

When you ask Disputr to generate suggested complaint wording, the complaint facts required for that request are sent to the configured AI service. Do not include information that is unnecessary for your complaint.

Evidence

Files you upload are stored privately and are intended to be available only through your authenticated case. You remain responsible for deciding what evidence to upload.

Security

Disputr uses authenticated sessions and stores password hashes rather than plain-text passwords. No online service can promise absolute security, so use a strong password that you do not reuse elsewhere.

Your questions

If you have a privacy or account question, use the contact page.
