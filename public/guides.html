Complaint guides

Use these guides to organise the facts, evidence and next steps around your complaint. They are general information only; the exact complaint route and deadlines can vary by provider and sector.

Finance

Banking, lending and financial services

Start with the product, account or agreement involved, what went wrong, when you first noticed it and what you have already asked the firm to do. Keep statements, agreements, screenshots and correspondence together. Regulated firms normally have their own complaints procedure and some disputes may later be eligible for the Financial Ombudsman Service. Check the firm's final response and current official eligibility rules before escalating.

Start a finance complaint

Gambling

Gambling operator complaints

Keep a clear timeline of deposits, withdrawals, account restrictions, communications and any safer-gambling interactions relevant to your complaint. Ask the operator to address each issue directly and keep its final response. If the dispute is eligible for alternative dispute resolution, the operator's complaints information should identify the relevant route.

Start a gambling complaint

Travel

Flights, rail and bookings

Record booking references, dates, scheduled and actual journey details, cancellation or delay information, costs you paid and the remedy you requested. Keep receipts and provider messages. Escalation routes differ between airlines, rail operators, booking platforms and other travel services, so check the provider's own complaints process first.

Start a travel complaint

Retail

Goods, services and refunds

Identify what you bought, when and how you paid, then describe the problem precisely. Keep proof of purchase, photographs, delivery information and messages with the seller. State the outcome you want clearly. If the retailer rejects the complaint, check any relevant payment-provider, ADR or court options before taking a further step.

Start a retail complaint

Energy & home

Energy, utilities and household services

Keep bills, meter readings, contracts, appointment records, photographs and correspondence. Put disputed charges or service failures into date order and explain what correction or resolution you want. Ombudsman or ADR routes vary by sector, so use the provider's formal complaint process and check the current official scheme before escalating.

Start a home or utilities complaint

Telecoms

Mobile, broadband and communications

Record the service, contract dates, faults or billing issues, contact history and any promised fixes. Keep bills, speed or outage evidence where relevant, and complaint reference numbers. Communications providers may belong to an approved ADR scheme; check the provider's current complaints information for the correct route.

Start a telecoms complaint

Parking

Private parking complaints and appeals

Keep the notice, photographs, payment records, signage evidence and any appeal correspondence. Check whether the notice is from a private parking company or a public authority because the processes differ. Follow the appeal instructions and applicable deadlines shown on the notice and use the relevant independent appeal route where available.

Start a parking complaint

General

Prepare any consumer complaint

Keep the complaint factual: what happened, when it happened, what you have already done, what evidence supports it and what outcome you want. Avoid adding facts you cannot evidence. Keep copies of everything you send and record dates and reference numbers so you can follow up properly.
