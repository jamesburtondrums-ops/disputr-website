Gambling complaints

Complaints about gambling operators

Separate the facts of the dispute from the outcome you want, and preserve the account history before it becomes difficult to reconstruct.

Does this guide apply?

Use this guide for complaints to a Great Britain licensed gambling business about gambling transactions, account handling, withdrawals, restrictions, customer service or relevant safer-gambling interactions.

Start by checking

  • The operator's complaints procedure
  • The account and transaction history
  • Relevant terms and conditions
  • Whether the business is licensed by the Gambling Commission

Evidence to collect

  • Deposit, withdrawal and transaction history
  • Account statements and relevant bank records
  • Chat transcripts, emails and complaint references
  • Responsible or safer-gambling communications where relevant
  • Dates of restrictions, closures or verification requests
  • Copies of the terms relied on by either side

State dates, times and amounts wherever possible and keep a copy of everything you give the operator.

Make the complaint to the operator first

Follow the operator's formal complaints procedure. Explain each complaint point separately and say what resolution you are seeking. The operator's entire internal complaints process should take no more than 8 weeks.

At the end of the process, or sooner if deadlock is reached, the operator should explain the final position and the relevant independent ADR route where the dispute is eligible.

ADR and the Gambling Commission are different

An approved Alternative Dispute Resolution provider can consider eligible unresolved disputes after the operator's process. The operator should tell you which ADR provider applies. The Gambling Commission regulates the industry but does not act as the decision-maker for individual consumer disputes.

Do not skip the operator processYou normally need to complete the gambling business's complaints procedure before an ADR provider can consider the dispute.

What makes a stronger escalation?

Provide the original complaint, the operator's responses, a short chronology, the disputed transactions and the documents that support each point. Explain exactly what remains unresolved rather than rewriting the complaint from scratch.
