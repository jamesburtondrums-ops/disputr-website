How it works

From problem to organised complaint.

Disputr is built to help you prepare, save and manage a complaint. It does not decide whether you are right or guarantee an outcome.

1

Choose the complaint area

Read the free guide first if you want context, then start a case when you are ready.

2

Record the facts

Add the company, issue, what happened and what outcome you want. Keep the wording factual.

3

Build suggested wording

Use the complaint draft tool as a starting point, then check and personalise everything before sending.

4

Keep evidence together

Upload supporting files and add timeline events so the case history stays in one place.

5

Track what happens next

Add reminders, update the case status and record responses from the business.

6

Consider escalation

If the business does not resolve the matter, check the relevant official ADR, ombudsman or appeal route for your sector.

What Disputr does not do

Disputr is a self-service complaint-management tool. It does not act as your solicitor, claims manager, ombudsman or representative. It cannot tell a business or external body what decision to make.
