How it works

A practical route from problem to prepared complaint.

The current public beta is free and does not require an account.

1

Choose a complaint type

Select the category and issue that most closely match what happened.

2

Select the provider

Search the directory or enter a company manually, then check its current official complaints route.

3

Add only checked facts

Provide dates, references, previous contact, evidenced costs and the outcome you want considered.

4

Review before sending

Edit the suggested wording, verify every fact, send it through the provider's official route and keep a copy.

Keep expectations clear

Your provider handles and decides your complaint under its own process. Disputr helps you prepare and organise it; it cannot decide the outcome.

Not legal advice.Check relevant official guidance and any applicable deadline for your own situation.
